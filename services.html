Shipping Policy

Safe Delivery, Guaranteed

We've perfected our live-fish shipping process over decades. Here's what to expect.

📋

Order Minimum

Live fish orders require a minimum of 4 fish to ensure safe water volume and temperature stability during transit.

📅

Ship Days

We ship live fish Monday through Wednesday to avoid fish sitting in transit over weekends. Delivery is next-day air.

Live Arrival Guarantee

We will replace or refund any discus that arrives DOA or unhealthy. Notify us with a photo within 2 hours of delivery — no questions asked.
