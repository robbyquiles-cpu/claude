FAQ

Common Questions

📦

How are the fish shipped?

Fish are triple-bagged with pure oxygen in insulated foam boxes. All live fish ship next-day air, Monday through Wednesday.

💳

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal, major credit cards, and Zelle. Payment is required in full before shipment.

🌡️

What water parameters do discus need?

Discus prefer soft, warm water: pH 6.0–7.0, temperature 82–86°F, and very low hardness. We include a care sheet with every order.

🔄

What is your return policy?

Live fish are non-returnable, but we honor our live-arrival guarantee. Contact us within 2 hours of delivery with photos for any DOA claims.
