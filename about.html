The Legacy

A Name Synonymous with Discus Excellence

Jack Wattley didn't just breed discus — he transformed an entire hobby and elevated it to an art form.

  • Founded in Pompano Beach, Florida in 1956 by Jack Wattley, a pioneer of modern discus genetics.
  • Jack developed the first commercially available turquoise discus, a breakthrough that changed the industry forever.
  • Author of the landmark book Handbook of Discus, still considered essential reading for serious hobbyists.
  • Decades of selective breeding have produced fish unmatched in color intensity, finnage, and body shape.
  • Today the tradition continues, maintaining Jack's rigorous standards with modern facilities and global reach.
