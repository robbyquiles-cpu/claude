The Legacy

A Name Synonymous with Discus Excellence

Jack Wattley didn't just breed discus — he transformed an entire hobby and elevated it to an art form.

  • Founded by Jack Wattley — the world's most renowned discus breeder — with nearly 60 years of experience developing captive-bred discus.
  • Jack developed the first commercially available turquoise discus, a breakthrough that changed the hobby forever.
  • Author of the landmark Handbook of Discus, still considered essential reading for serious hobbyists worldwide.
  • Gabriel Posada has been running the Miami hatchery since 1997, continuing Jack's legacy with the same passion and standards.
  • Located at 6944 NE 4th Ave, Miami, FL 33138 — serving customers globally through shipping and local visitors by appointment.
Our Mission

Passion for the Hobby, Commitment to the Fish

We believe every discus enthusiast — beginner or expert — deserves access to healthy, beautiful fish backed by real expertise. As passionate hobbyists ourselves, we provide the finest quality discus available, with expert advice always just a call, text, or email away.

🔬

Research-Driven Breeding

Every spawn is guided by decades of documented genetic knowledge. Our Miami hatchery uses reverse osmosis water to replicate ideal Amazonian conditions.

🌿

Wild & Captive-Bred

We offer both captive-bred strains and wild-caught varieties. All fish are raised in optimal conditions and health-checked before shipping.

🤝

Community First

We invest in the discus community through education, publications, and partnerships with clubs and societies worldwide.
